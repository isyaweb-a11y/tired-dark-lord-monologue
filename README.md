A monologue of about a hundred words from a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The weariness lives in the pauses and the small chores of villainy, never in a plain announcement: "You know what the hero never asks? Who sharpens the swords. Who feeds the dragon. Who files the conquest in triplicate."

`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` when run.
